## Release objectives

1. Take one eligible Creator and Editor from clear commercial terms to accepted delivery, provider-mediated electronic payment and an eligible review.
2. Offer both Jobs and Services by the public MVP. Preserve the transaction origin and use common agreement, project, delivery, payment and dispute controls.
3. Use the controlled Pilot to measure completion, payment exceptions, support load and repeat use. Confirm appropriate staffing and transparent moderation before broadening access.

## Pilot path decision

| Option | Demonstrates | Cost and dependency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [PROPOSED] Jobs-first Pilot | Validates hiring, payment and delivery through one entry path | Separately validate service search, listing versioning and request acceptance before public MVP |
| Jobs + Services Pilot | Tests both acquisition paths and confirms shared transaction rules earlier | Requires expanded offer and withdrawal policy, extra verification and refund acceptance cases, and staff coverage before Pilot entry |

D-017 owns the Pilot choice. The scope approval for the public MVP remains recorded in D-001. The proposed Pilot provides a controlled learning milestone and carries its evidence into the public release review.

## Release criteria and evidence

| Gate | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Technical Pilot | Enabled paths reach acceptance and controlled closure using approved synthetic or sandbox data. Duplicate hire, duplicate financial event, unauthorized file access and interrupted upload cases pass. Support queue, audit and incident routing are exercised |
| Sensitive-data use | D-011 data inventory, legal/processing review, access and retention controls are approved before handling real verification or confidential project media |
| Real-money Pilot | D-005/010 provider and counsel evidence, approved fee and funds-flow policies, refund/dispute runbook, operational owners, reliable reconciliation and tested loss/retry scenarios |
| Public MVP | Both Jobs and Services are tested through the shared lifecycle. Required quality targets and Arabic/RTL are validated on both paths. Verification criteria, staffing and policy ownership are confirmed. Formal launch approval records scope and open issues |

No calendar commitment was made. D-013 establishes measurable service, accessibility, backup and metric targets. D-012 establishes commercial assumptions and fee evidence.

## Subsequent product phases

Phase 2 candidates include richer discovery, repeat-work tools, verification automation, operational analytics and paid tiers subject to product evidence. Phase 3 candidates include B2B, approved geographic expansion and separately evaluated financial or AI capabilities. Each addition needs its own scope, operating impact and acceptance evidence.
